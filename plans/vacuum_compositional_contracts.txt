4. Implement PSD sampled classical Gaussian displacement covariance, calibrated
   coupling and signed pulse quadrature. Test Ramsey, ideal quasistatic echo,
   zero coupling, invalid covariance and relaxation. Keep ordered quantum noise
   on its separately declared contract.
5. Prove the corresponding finite-dimensional storage identity, supply bound,
   Gaussian variance nonnegativity, coherence range and echo cancellation.
   Prove scalar Choi admissibility separately from classical passivity.

   the scalar proper-time dimension, self-energy subtraction and Brownian
   measure from the primary source. A finite stochastic output cannot close
   normalization or convergence obligations. Retain explicit residuals.
7. Add a deterministic composition example and a machine-checked 35-leaf
   integration manifest. Check witness paths/declarations and reject stale,
   duplicate, missing or unjustifiably closed physical rows.
8. Run focused Rust tests/Clippy, complete multicore proof compilation,
   admission scan, kernel replay and assumption inventories. Independently
   review, run hosted CI, merge, synchronize canonical main and retain or
   archive worktrees according to measured outstanding edits.

------------

- Classical covariance and ordered quantum spectra have different contracts.
- Real-valued proofs do not certify floating-point execution.
- Finite-dimensional/truncated results do not establish infinite-field limits.
- Source recovery and model validation do not establish a measured device.
- The canonical material/provenance/statistical owners remain the owners of
  specimen joins and matched-control inference; the new model must compose
  through those owners rather than duplicate their vocabularies.
