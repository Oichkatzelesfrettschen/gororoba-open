Canonical code ownership
------------------------

cd_kernel::cayley_dickson::boolean_permutation owns validated Boolean
label words, complete finite permutations, inverse maps and affine
coefficient extraction. boolean_phase owns value-oracle interpolation and
the exact Walsh measurement boundary. algebra_analysis::exact_annihilator
owns exact left-action matrix regression witnesses.

TargetCube predicates exclude the target and constrain only data bits.
TargetFlipWord validates its full word before evaluation. Data widths are
one through five; bit data_width is the outer control. Every disabled
control branch fixes its label. Reversing the word gives its inverse.
The retained N21 words cover multipliers 2, 4 and 16, all 32 data labels,
identity padding at labels 21 through 31, and both control values. Tests
cover 192 controlled labels and all 51 single-cube deletions. The primitive
test enumerates all 547 admitted cubes at widths 1-5 and checks their
controlled involution action on every input.

SignedXorTranslation retains the output label and diagonal sign as
separate values. Translation zero fixes every label while a nonzero sign
exponent can still change the operator. The fixed-zero obstruction is an
identity-label statement; it does not erase diagonal phases or identify
the full multiplication group with a direct product.

affine_skeleton reads the constant and degree-one ANF coefficients of a
complete bijection. Its GF(2) rank need not be full and need not agree with
the rank of the functional inverse. Identity-padded doubling modulo 21
has linear rank 5; its inverse multiplier 11 has linear rank 4. The port
retains this exact counterexample without importing a residual optimizer,
an optimal CNOT search, or a compiler-performance claim.

recover_degree_promised_anf requires every low-weight query input exactly
once and rejects missing, duplicate or extra samples. Its domain is
0 <= d <= n <= 8. Unique recovery relies on an external degree promise.
A degree d+1 monomial vanishes at every queried input of weight <= d and
aliases the zero polynomial; the API cannot certify an unseen oracle's
degree. Coefficient-basis and dense tests retain query counts 4, 11, 26
and 57 at (n,d)=(3,1),(4,2),(5,3),(6,4); constant and complete
eight-variable edge cases are checked separately.

The translated cubics f=x0*x1*x2 and g=x1*x2+x0*x1*x2 differ and are not
complements, yet their squared Walsh spectra agree at n=3 and n=5.
The collision concerns the uniform-input Walsh probability measurement.
OracleAccess::UncontrolledPhase retains its existing full-access
equivalence up to complementation; its representatives distinguish the
two cubics. The existing BooleanPhaseError enum is unchanged; the added
interpolation API has a separate DegreePromisedAnfError type.

The exact matrix tests reuse multiplication_matrix_exact. For octonions,
L_(e1*e2)e4=e7 but L_e1 L_e2 e4=-e7. For sedenions, x=e1+e10 and
y=e4-e15 satisfy x*y=0 and x*x=-2e0, so L_x L_x y=0 whereas
L_(x*x)y=-2y. Reversing the probe sign gives a nonzero product. These
witnesses refute multiplicative and Jordan-square readings of the left
action; they do not claim a geometry or a different algebra product.
