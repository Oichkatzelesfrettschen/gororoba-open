Held-out evaluation of P13d: statement written before the run
================================================================

Source of the registration: pull request 288 (merge commit da4628ea), the
section "P13d (registered here, before any held-out run)", and the module
documentation of forecast_null.rs. This file restates it before the run and
changes nothing in it.

Prediction (P13d). On configurations held out of P13a to P13c, the associator
does not beat its baselines against a null calibrated to the data.

Unit. A (run, quartile) pair of the anchored forecast with at least 10
positives and both classes; quartiles of the smallest population share at the
anchor.

Statement. In at most half of the pairs does the associator's
better-orientation ROC area exceed both thresholds: (a) the 0.95 quantile of
the exchangeable-label null over the symmetry orbits of that pair (the
rank-sum law of the orbit counts), which replaces the fixed 0.65; (b) the
largest better-orientation area among the five product-free scores (Gram
volume, step difference, Mach number, smallest population share, |c - a|)
plus 0.02.

Falsifier. More than half of the pairs exceed both thresholds. With fewer
than 8 pairs the run reports undecided and no verdict is read. A falsified
P13d means the associator carries forecast information beyond the baselines
and the orbit null on held-out data; the torus, surrogate-increment and
algebra-substitution results of the same run then decide whether the product
is credited.

Skip rules. A pair whose associator area, orbit threshold or any
product-free area is not finite receives no call, is listed under skipped
with its reason, and leaves the pair count and the floor of 8. A pair with an
orbit that holds both labels receives no call, is listed under skipped, and
leaves the pair count and the floor of 8.

Decision rule as coded (p13d_verdict, p13d_decide): a pair calls when the
associator oriented area exceeds max(threshold, best product-free + 0.02);
the verdict is undecided when fewer than 8 pairs are decided (a decided
pair is one not listed under skipped), held when calls * 2 <= decided pairs,
failed otherwise. The direct-relabeling threshold is recorded beside the
rank-sum threshold and does not set the verdict.

Held-out runs (tau, U0, delta, first violating step; eps 0.05), the kept rows
of the retained scan records that no score list holds:
  (0.5005, 0.16, 2, 261)
  (0.5005, 0.16, 4, 2727)

  (0.5005, 0.3, 4, 323)
  (0.502, 0.25, 4, 447)
  (0.502, 0.3, 4, 332)

Development value of the rule on the nine runs: 1 of 26 pairs (calibration,
not a test).

run from the repository root with every other option at its default

paths unchanged).
