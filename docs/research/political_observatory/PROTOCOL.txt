POLITICAL OBSERVATORY: EMPIRICAL CONTRACT
Research specification, 2026-11-03. Status: source discovery and curated pilot.
No fitted comprehensive political model, local opinion estimate, 32D advantage,
quantum speedup, causal effect or litigation success rate is established.

1. Scope and outputs

Build and validate in order: Pasadena city; Los Angeles County; California;
Texas; Kansas; then additional regions and national synthesis. National inputs
can enter the local observation model as shared context before national outputs
are authorized by this sequence. The five requested areas are a purposive
comparison, not a representative sample of the United States.

Retain separate outputs for material conditions, perceived conditions, policy
preferences, institutional trust, participation, collective action, legal
outcomes, recovery and electoral outcomes. Do not collapse them into one
unvalidated political-health score. For each output report observed versus
modeled values, coverage, missingness, revision age and uncertainty.

The initial pilot contains explicitly curated numerical facts from primary
summary pages, not full raw survey or event datasets. Every observation uses
retrieval as a conservative availability bound; none is admitted to a historical
forecast merely because its fieldwork predates that forecast. Published dates
are metadata, not proof that today's revised content existed on that date.
Retain publication intervals, first-seen timestamps and source bytes in future
adapters. Do not backdate revisions. Zero election counts before reporting are
unreported, not voter preference or a zero-percent share.

2. Measured domain inventory

Preference and salience: candidate choices, office approval, issue importance,
policy support, confidence, perceived representation, political efficacy.
Material conditions: prices by category, real pay, employment, hours, job loss,
business entry/closure, bankruptcy, credit access, arrears and debt transitions.
Purchasing: nominal and category-deflated expenditure, quantities where
available, discretionary/essential mix, income-group differences, local taxable
sales, merchant-location versus resident-location spending.
Mobility and travel: TSA screenings, airport enplanements, hotel nights,
transit boardings, traffic, commute costs and airport connectivity. Airport
passengers are not necessarily local residents or discretionary tourists.
Housing and recovery: rent burden, evictions, homelessness, insurance
nonrenewals, premiums, rebuild permits, disaster aid and displacement.
Collective action: strikes, lockouts, labor protests, demonstrations,
participation ranges, worker-days, bargaining outcomes and exposure duration.
Institutions and law: case cohorts, standing, relief sought, stages,
injunctions, merits decisions, stays, reversals, settlements, compliance,
appeals and time at risk.
Information: topic salience, source diversity, narrative disagreement,
syndication, language, platform activity and coordinated amplification.
Representation and administration: electoral geography, registration,
ballot access, campaign spending, public budgets, procurement, council
decisions, service requests and documented delivery.
Context: weather, disaster exposure, public-health disruption, school
attendance, education costs, utilities, water stress, immigration enforcement,
international trade/tourism exposure and foreign public opinion.

Sources.json enumerates inspected source surfaces. It does not establish
complete coverage, live credentials, unrestricted redistribution or successful
bulk acquisition. Expansion candidates remain in coverage.json with their
unresolved admission steps.

3. Geography, population and translation

No county value becomes a measured city value. Pasadena, Altadena, South
Pasadena, Pasadena Unified School District and the Pasadena Continuum of Care
are distinct. The LA CPI area includes Orange County. LAHSA's LA Continuum of
Care is not identical to LA County; reconcile Pasadena's separate coverage.
School districts, council districts, sewersheds, ZIPs, ZCTAs and precincts
require versioned crosswalks, not name matching.

Use authoritative boundary vintages and stable codes. Preserve the source CRS.
No geometry is stored or transformed by the pilot. Future area measurement
requires an appropriate projected/equal-area CRS; never use Web Mercator for
area weighting. Population-weighted crosswalks require documented source
counts and uncertainty; area weighting alone assumes uniform density.
Reconcile one-to-many boundary joins and report unmatched records. A city
estimate derived from county change assumptions is modeled, not independent
city evidence; EDD's subcounty employment method makes this distinction
especially consequential.

Foreign opinion has its own population and measurement model. Country medians
are not population-weighted global estimates and never enter the US vote
likelihood as US preferences. Test foreign confidence, trade, tourism and
domestic exposure relationships as contextual hypotheses. Preserve survey
language, original prompt, response scale, source-language text, translated
text, translator/version and adjudication status. Back-translate and compare
meaning for any cross-language item admitted to pooled estimation. Spanish
catalog terminology is summarized in literature.json; questionnaire equivalence
has not been established.

4. Hierarchical model and temporal measurement

For domain d, geography g and time t, define a domain-specific latent state
z[d,g,t]. Use an explicit observation equation
 y[j,g,t] = h[j](z[d,g,t]) + source_effect[j] + method_effect[j,t] + error[j,g,t].
Allow source-cluster and cycle-cluster dependence. Survey likelihoods must
respect weighted estimates, design effects and respondent overlap.
Aggregated percentages are not binomial counts formed by multiplying a rounded
percentage by total sample size.

A dynamic-factor or state-space layer handles ragged releases and mixed
frequencies. Weekly card spending, monthly prices and quarterly debt remain
at their measured frequency. No interpolation creates new independent data.
Estimate factor loadings and scaling inside training folds; missingness,
staleness and release surprises remain explicit.

Geographic hierarchy: city effects nested in county and state where genuinely
nested, plus shared national factors. Non-nested geographies require explicit
cross-classification. Partial pooling does not make a sparse city estimate a
direct observation. Dynamic MRP is an optional opinion layer only after
respondent microdata and suitable poststratification counts are acquired and
diagnostics support identification. Public toplines alone do not supply MRP.

Separate mechanisms from prediction. A causal effect of a protest, strike,
court ruling or price shock needs a predeclared estimand, timing, comparison
group, confounder strategy and interference assessment. Event studies require
pretrend checks and sensitivity; association and forecast gain are insufficient.

5. Litigation and collective-action outcomes

Predeclare separate legal cohorts: election administration, executive-policy
review, labor, housing/consumer, and local governance. Record unit as case,
consolidated case-family or requested claim/relief. Do not mix them.
A preliminary-relief grant is not a final merits win. Dismissal for standing
does not establish substantive legality. A settlement has separately coded
terms, not an automatic win. Appeals can change effective outcome.

Report:
 - preliminary relief granted / eligible relief motions resolved by cutoff;
 - favorable merits outcomes / classified merits dispositions by cutoff;
 - stay/reversal rates among review-eligible rulings;
 - time-to-resolution with pending cases censored;
 - realized compliance separately from judicial orders.
Publish numerator, denominator, unclassified and pending counts. FJC
administrative codes require codebook review and case-document adjudication;
RECAP's selection limits population claims. No success rates exist in the
pilot because no adjudicated case cohort has been acquired.

For strikes, distinguish workers, sites, actions, duration, lockouts and
settlement terms. Deduplicate multi-site disputes while retaining site exposure.
BLS's >=1000-worker series cannot stand in for all strikes. Protest events
require event-level deduplication across news articles and separate uncertainty
for attendance. No count becomes support for a party without additional data.

6. Social and news measurement

Use permitted public aggregate/research access. Preserve sampling queries,
time windows, returned coverage, language and platform. Do not infer an
individual's political belief or precise residence from posts, names or
demographic proxies. No person-level persuasion or targeting.
Topic prevalence, sentiment, stance, sarcasm and factuality are distinct labels.
Annotate a geographically and linguistically stratified validation sample;
report intercoder disagreement and per-language confusion matrices.
Do not call automated-account scores proof of bots. Use sensitivity analyses
with and without high-volume and coordinated clusters.
Separate resident discussion from outsiders discussing a place. Non-geolocated
content remains unknown geography. Foreign-language content is not foreign
influence. Handle deleted posts through permitted retention/deletion procedures.

GDELT is a media-observation layer. ACLED is an event-coded layer. Cornell and
BLS have different labor-action coverage. Syndicated articles are deduplicated
before topic-frequency estimation, and event records link multiple sources.
Unavailable data remains a gap; registration-required sources are not scraped
around their access requirements.

7. Repository mathematics as competing features

First fit baselines: seasonal/persistence, hierarchical lagged regression,
dynamic factor, conventional nonlinear interactions, covariance/rank and
matched-window change-point diagnostics. No predictive readiness before EDA,
missingness, target balance, dependence and temporal coverage are reported.

Candidate 32D construction: eight aligned channels x four declared delays.
This is a feature construction, not proof of intrinsic political dimension.
Declare basis order, centering, scales, receptive field and all maps.
Feature groups must be selected within nested training, never because they
fit today's narrative. Monitor measurement/calendar artifacts.

Compare the associator against:
 - temporal and block controls;
 - phase and multivariate cross-spectral controls with preservation tested;
 - fixed-support sign-scramble ensembles, not one favorable seed;
 - alternate algebras and generic cubic contractions at matched support;
 - dimension and basis-placement ablations;
 - identical history, parameter budget and tuning budget.
Zero divisors motivate observability and cancellation hypotheses; a feature
multiplication zero is not a political mechanism. Use empirical Jacobian/SVD
analyses for aggregate cancellation and report weak identification.

The current crossing-detector report records a small positive archive AUC
increment whose interval lies below its declared 0.005 target, and 14-16 of
19 fixed-support sign controls equal or exceed canonical estimates. Preserve
this finding. Historical 32D reports do not override later matched controls.
Quantum implementations compare exact output laws and classical equivalents,
including loading, compilation, execution and decoding cost. No quantum run,
credit spending or speedup claim is part of this initial observatory.

8. Validation and sequential gates

G0: acquire source bytes, terms, schema, timestamps and coverage; enumerate all
expected polls/waves per admitted provider/date interval. Reconcile duplicates,
excluded records, missing resources and access failures. "Exhaustive" means
complete relative to that declared provider x geography x period inventory.
G1: build city/county historical panels and direct-vs-modeled evidence cards.
G2: validate local descriptive estimates, then state layers CA -> TX -> KS.
G3: evaluate political outcomes and diagnostic features on chronological
held-out cycles plus held-out geographies and sources.
G4: expand to a representative national panel and nationally reconcile totals.
Do not sum Pasadena and LA County or CA with its counties. Counts aggregate
only over disjoint units; rates aggregate using their own denominators.
G5: frozen prospective assessment with report provenance and monitoring.

All imputation/scaling/selection is training-only. Purge overlapping windows
and labels; retain election-cycle clusters. A hundred districts in one cycle
are not a hundred independent national experiments. Compare proper scores,
MAE, calibration, interval coverage, source robustness and domain ablations.
Report paired differences and uncertainty at defensible dependence units.
For descriptive diagnostics preregister nulls and correct search multiplicity.
Model attribution is predictive decomposition, not causal attribution.

A useful feature survives only for its demonstrated task. If generic cubic
controls match the CD features, retain useful feature engineering but abandon
algebra-specific advantage. If no held-out improvement survives, keep the
descriptive source system and reject predictive augmentation.

9. Storage, operation and completion

The separate SQLite schema is an observation ledger, not the canonical claim
registry. No registry claim IDs are minted here. JSON provenance remains
inspectable. Raw third-party files stay out of git when redistribution terms,
size or personal data prohibit inclusion; retain hashes and retrieval recipes.

The first implementation is a Rust Cargo example using existing workspace
dependencies. It audits the curated bundle, filters by availability cutoff,
emits coverage and computes only explicitly declared arithmetic contrasts.
It neither fetches all providers nor fits the proposed models. SQLite schema
and curated records were checked separately in the session. Rust is absent in
the execution environment; compile/test evidence must come from hosted CI or
a Rust-equipped checkout. Do not label this as a validated production model.

Required next acquisition: historical LA community/precinct returns and
boundary crosswalks; Pasadena taxable sales/recovery series; permitted LA
survey microdata; then CA/TX/KS polling wave inventories and economic histories.
No automation, external email, paid data acquisition or deployment is created.
