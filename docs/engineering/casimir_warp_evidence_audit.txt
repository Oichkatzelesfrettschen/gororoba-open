CASIMIR STRESS AND WARP-SOURCE EVIDENCE

Scope
=====

This audit separates an ideal electromagnetic stress reference, a scalar
worldline interaction calculation, a prescribed spacetime and a physical
measurement. The executable supplies a local counterexample to identifying
a gravitational source from energy density alone. It does not reproduce the
White 2021 sphere-cylinder calculation or construct a finite warp bubble.

The quantum repository owns the historical source assessment and QPU
interpretation in docs/notes/topics/casimir-warp-evidence.md. The Rust
implementation owns the numerical contract and derivation below.

Existing work and remaining gap
==============================

The scalar worldline implementation on the parent revision already uses the
3+1-dimensional real-scalar coefficient -1/(32*pi^2), a distinct-two-body
interaction indicator and a nonnegative Monte Carlo standard error. Its
integrated plate benchmark separates scalar and electromagnetic coefficients.
The older quantum handoff records retain their historical meaning; they are
not a current defect list.

The remaining source question concerns the gravitational interpretation of
the computed density. A loop-center effective-action integrand differs from
local physical T_00 by a total derivative (Gies and Klingmueller 2006, p. 8,
footnote 3). A scalar boundary model also does not generally supply an
electromagnetic stress tensor for arbitrary geometries. The energy.rs and
lib.rs contracts now retain those distinctions.

Ideal electromagnetic reference
===============================

For zero temperature, infinite perfectly conducting plates and SI separation
a, define K = pi^2*hbar*c/(720*a^4). In an orthonormal frame with signature
(-,+,+,+), and plates normal to x, the vacuum contribution is

  T_ab = K diag(-1,-3,+1,+1).

Consequently rho=-K, the normal pressure is -3K, tangential pressures are K,
and E/A=-K*a. The Lorentzian trace vanishes and normal pressure equals
-d(E/A)/da. Density and pressure scale as a^-4; area energy scales as a^-3.

The checked API in casimir_core::stress_energy supports all three normal
axes, normalized null directions and subluminal observers. Tests include
the virtual-work derivative, tensor orientation, scalar/EM factor two for
parallel plates, scale powers and floating-point range failures. These
identities are analytic reference tests, not empirical material validation.

The tensor excludes plate rest energy, supports, exterior fields, finite
conductivity, thermal corrections and edges. Those contributions cannot be
omitted when solving for the geometry of a complete apparatus.

Exact local gravitational counterexample
=======================================

Use x0=c*t, a dimensionless smooth function B(y), and the stationary metric

  ds^2 = -(dx0)^2 + [dx-B(y) dx0]^2 + dy^2 + dz^2.

Its Eulerian orthonormal vectors are e_0=(1,B,0,0), e_x=(0,1,0,0),
e_y=(0,0,1,0), e_z=(0,0,0,1). Set q=dB/dy and r=d^2B/dy^2.
The metric coefficients are g_00=-1+B^2, g_0x=-B, g_ii=1.
The determinant is -1 and the inverse has g^00=-1, g^0x=-B,
g^xx=1-B^2, g^yy=g^zz=1.

Substitute those coefficients into

  Gamma^a_bc = (g^ad/2)(partial_b g_dc + partial_c g_db - partial_d g_bc),
  R_bd = partial_a Gamma^a_bd - partial_d Gamma^a_ba
         + Gamma^a_ae Gamma^e_bd - Gamma^a_de Gamma^e_ba,
  G_bd = R_bd - g_bd R/2.

Transformation with e_a^mu gives the covariant orthonormal tensor

  G_00=-q^2/4, G_0x=G_x0=r/2, G_xx=-3q^2/4,
  G_yy=+q^2/4, G_zz=-q^2/4,

with all other components zero and R=q^2/2. Its Lorentzian trace is -R.
gr_core::warp_stress_energy evaluates this exact tensor with finite/range
checks. An independent finite-difference metric-curvature test reconstructs
all sixteen entries; this is separate from checking the closed formula's
internal identities.

Let kappa=8*pi*G/c^4. Set r=0 and q=2*sqrt(kappa*K), using the ideal plate
reference above. The 00, xx and yy components match kappa*T, but

  G_zz-kappa*T_zz = -2*kappa*K.

Thus equality of energy density does not imply the Einstein equation.
The disagreement persists after normalization by kappa*K and remains -2
at every supported gap, up to floating-point roundoff. The construction is local: it imposes
no finite-bubble boundary, material realization, stability or propulsion.
The matched q is a derivative chosen to exhibit the logical counterexample;
it is not a measured or inferred warp speed.

Metric regression corrections
=============================

NacelleWarpParams::alcubierre specifies the unmodulated spherical profile.
The zero-nacelle shift now omits the additional axial taper, which previously
altered that profile away from the midplane. Its energy-density regression
uses the analytic spherical-profile derivative at an off-midplane point.

For the implemented ADM convention g_0i=beta_i and lapse one, the Eulerian
normal has components (1,-beta). Its expansion is -div(beta). The expansion
function now agrees with that sign and with the existing ADM computation.

The finite-width nacelle and Cayley-Dickson envelope remain phenomenological
model choices. A logistic gate and tanh profile do not certify a finite
exactly flat interior or vanishing tidal forces. Naming a constructor after
White 2025 does not establish quantitative replication of that paper.

Reproduction and output contract
================================

Use the repository's pinned toolchain. Run these named-surface diagnostics:

  cargo test --locked -p casimir_core --lib stress_energy
  cargo test --locked -p gr_core --lib warp_
  cargo test --locked -p gororoba_cli_physics --bin casimir-warp-audit
  cargo run --locked -p gororoba_cli_physics --bin casimir-warp-audit -- \
    --gaps-m 1e-6,4e-6 --output-dir data/output/audit/casimir-warp-evidence

Set CARGO_TARGET_DIR to an absolute directory. Local diagnosis leaves worker
counts at native defaults. GitHub Actions remains the admission authority.
The CLI uses Rayon for independent cases and ordered collection for output.
No provider, random seed, service or QPU allocation is required.

reference_scalars.tsv carries SI density, area energy, normal pressure and
the source-strength scale kappa*K in m^-2. tensor_residuals.tsv retains all
sixteen entries of G, T, G-kappa*T and its normalization. summary.json binds
the units, assumptions, classification and unresolved physical requirements.
The two positive gaps are ideal parallel-plate examples. Their numerical
sizes do not turn them into the White sphere-cylinder geometry.

The source-strength scale is not a solved curvature invariant. No column
reports a laboratory propagation delay, experimental signal or warp speed.
The CLI rejects subnormal source scales because they cannot preserve the
relative precision required by the normalized tensor-residual contract.

Physical requirements beyond this implementation
================================================

Obtain a renormalized local stress tensor for the actual boundaries,
materials and quantum state. Include the apparatus, conservation law and
consistent gravitational boundary/initial data. Solve the source/geometry
problem and derive an observable with uncertainties. Discriminate ordinary
dispersion, waveguide response, electronic delay and instrument drift with
calibrated controls. A physical transit-time difference alone does not select
gravity as its cause. Encoded QPU dynamics establish properties of their
declared simulation, not spacetime curvature in a separate apparatus.
