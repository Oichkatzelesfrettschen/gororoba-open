Finite-graph full heat trace, version 2
======================================

Defect and correction
---------------------
The former heat_kernel_return_probability omitted zero Laplacian eigenvalues
and normalized by the number of positive modes. That is a relaxation trace,
not the uniformly averaged finite-graph return probability. The corrected
function includes every zero mode. evaluate_heat_trace returns the declared
convention, zero-mode count, retained count, probability, log probability and
analytic logarithmic derivative. positive_mode_relaxation_trace exposes the
old normalization under its correct name.

The derivative is 2t times the exponentially weighted mean eigenvalue. A
shifted sum remains stable after probabilities underflow. Infinite positive
exponents contribute zero weight and zero moment; invalid negative or
non-finite spectra, invalid times and unrepresentable derivatives are refused.
The grid uses log interpolation rather than t_max/t_min, which could overflow.
No smoothing or finite difference of log(0) enters the corrected curve.

Two vertices with spectrum (0,2) give P=(1+exp(-2t))/2 and
D_s=4t/(1+exp(2t)), tending to zero at both ends of diffusion time. The
positive-mode relaxation gives exp(-2t) and D_s=4t instead. A finite cycle
can approximate dimension one at intermediate times and still approaches
zero at sufficiently late times. Neither result measures physical spacetime.

Scope and migration
-------------------
zd_laplacian_eigenvalues continues to analyze the first cross-assessor
component. Its documentation now says so instead of claiming a union. Other
components are counted, not assumed isospectral. DimensionResult retains the
full representative spectrum including zero modes. A complete disconnected
union remains a separate computation when components differ.

PlateauFit.flatness_score replaces the incorrect r_squared name. The score
is max(0,1-std/abs(mean)), not a regression coefficient of determination.
The longest-flat-window selector is descriptive: late-time finite-size
saturation may win and is not a continuum dimensional plateau. The old
is_expander field remains a finite-graph heuristic, not a theorem about a
uniform expanding family. The CLI labels that boundary and validates its
dimension ladder, including zero and overflow cases.

The CLI writes distinct full_trace_v2 filenames and a convention file; it
does not overwrite historical CSVs, generated registries or proof receipts.
Existing claims and exported plateaus need a source-bound successor replay
before they can refer to the corrected definition. Historical data are not
silently relabeled or re-adjudicated by this change.

Validation
----------
Thirteen native library tests and one CLI ladder test cover analytic two-node
and complete graphs, disconnected limits, isolated nodes, a cycle's intermediate
window, scaling identities, positive-mode underflow, invalid inputs, the
representative zero mode and polynomial smoothing. The smoothing helper uses
SVD rather than normal equations and no longer enters the spectral estimator.

An independent deterministic NumPy/analytic oracle checked 138 cases; the
maximum complete-graph absolute discrepancy was 4.440892098500626e-16. This
checks the formulas, not Rust compilation. The implementation container lacks
rustc/cargo/rustfmt. Native test, clippy, formatting, caller closure and census
replay are merge prerequisites. No cosmological inference or provider run is
introduced.
