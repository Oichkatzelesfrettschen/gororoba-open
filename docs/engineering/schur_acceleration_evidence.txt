Centered Schur acceleration evidence
===================================

Second-order compatibility separates a curved constant-nullity path from a
straight path with the same velocity. The algebra_analysis::schur_acceleration
module ports the exact certificate mechanism from gororoba-quantum's
schur_acceleration.py into the existing rational multiplication and annihilator
infrastructure. The quantum source snapshot is
95fdc7f2dc25316eb2ba7952a1a5f65c640ec6b2. The retained source manifest binds the
production inputs, proof note and independent Python replay by SHA-256.

Execution domain and centered frame
----------------------------------

The public chart domain has dimension 32. Center coordinates are exact
BigRational values, coordinates 0 and 16 vanish, the center is nonzero, and
its left-multiplication nullity is 4, 8, 12 or 16. Directions and accelerations
use all 30 remaining coordinates and may be zero. Rational centers retain
their own sphere radius; an irrational unit normalization is unnecessary.
Invalid dimensions, malformed rational denominators, purity violations,
unsupported center nullities and singular pivots return explicit errors.

CenteredSchurChart rebuilds L_center through multiplication_matrix_exact and
retains an AnnihilatorCertificate. Independent image columns form U, and
the complete certified kernel forms V. Verification checks the exact source
operator, the kernel and image frames, skew symmetry, U^T V=0, and both
inverse identities for A0=U^T L_center U. These checks establish an invertible
combined frame. schur_at admits a point only when its fixed pivot is invertible.

For the skew block convention [[A,B],[-B^T,D]], the residual is

  S(x)=D(x)+B(x)^T A(x)^(-1) B(x).

Block elimination gives rank L_x=(32-k)+rank S(x) on that pivot domain.
A zero residual therefore has the center's nullity k. The plus sign follows
from skew symmetry; changing the sign changes the necessary accelerations.
Biss, Christensen, Dugger and Isaksen, Lemma 2.3, grounds the adjoint identity.
The quantum proof note records the real differentiation argument and its
source attribution.

Complete acceleration certificates
----------------------------------

The module reconstructs every derivative direction V^T L_ei V, then keeps
the strictly upper-triangular entries as Dmap. For a velocity h, it forms

  Q(h)=B(h)^T A0^(-1) B(h),
  M=[Dmap; center^T],
  rhs=[upper_triangle(-2Q(h)); -||h||^2].

Direct operator entries and the center inner product reconstruct the
first-order residual. FirstOrderInadmissible carries that nonzero residual.
SecondOrderObstructed carries ell with ell^T M=0 and ell^T rhs!=0.
SecondOrderCompatible carries a particular acceleration and a full
homogeneous kernel. Each status requires its own witness and excludes the
other witness type.

The affine space retains a Schur particular solution a0, the radial correction
alpha=(-||h||^2-center^T a0)/||center||^2, and a=a0+alpha*center. The verifier
checks Dmap*center=0, so the correction preserves the Schur equation while
enforcing the norm equation. The norm row increases rank by one; every
separating covector has coefficient zero on that row.

A nonzero rank-sized minor proves the system's lower rank bound. The retained
kernel has 30-rank independent columns, witnessed by an identity block on
the free rows, and M*K=0 proves the matching upper bound. Every real solution
is therefore particular+K*z. The Rust evaluation interface accepts rational
parameters and returns all 32 ambient coordinates. verify_member independently
checks V^T L_acceleration V+2Q(h)=0 and the second norm equation.

SchurAccelerationCertificate::verify reconstructs the source chart, all 30
derivative directions and the velocity equations. The verifier checks exact
identities and retained witnesses without calling the acceleration constructor.
Malformed shapes and denominators fail before retained-field arithmetic.

Finite replay and falsifiers
---------------------------

The eleven module tests cover four exact centers with nullities 4, 8, 12 and
16, all 30 derivative directions at each center, complete zero-velocity
acceleration spaces, radial rejection and dense rational first-order directions.
The nonzero curvature fixture at center e1+e10 uses velocity e3. Its derivative
map has rank 6, the sphere system has rank 7, and the affine acceleration space
has dimension 23. Acceleration -e1 verifies. Zero acceleration, a wrong sign,
a missing factor two and radial-only acceleration fail the direct equations.

The incompatible fixture at e1+e18 uses velocity e11. The derivative map has
rank 8 and the first-order sphere kernel has dimension 21. The residual rows
(0,1) and (4,5) have equal coefficients and right-hand sides 2 and -2.
Their difference yields a separating covector with pairing -4. The obstruction
excludes a second-order jet in the named same-nullity locus.

Four rational samples of the source curvature path retain norm squared two
and nullity eight; the matching straight samples have full-rank Schur residual
and determinant t^16. The t=2 curve point e3+e10 remains inside the chosen
centered pivot domain. The point e1-e10 has a directly checked singular pivot.
These samples establish finite exact regression results. The source's symbolic
path identity remains separately retained in the quantum evidence bundle.

The tests reject 62 altered-evidence or reassigned-status cases across chart,
derivative, equation, affine-space, separator and malformed-rational fields.
Invalid public input shapes and purity violations receive separate error checks.
Negative rational center rescalings and quadratic velocity rescalings preserve
the exact normalization contract.
