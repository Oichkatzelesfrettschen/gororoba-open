Theorem and observable binding
------------------------------
Every scoped declaration names its source digest, coefficient domain, basis,
normalization, decoder, observable, execution class and required premises.
Execution classes and premises are selected by declaration. General weighted
probability, Bernoulli-moment, complex-number and radius support lemmas mark
physical fields inapplicable. The 32-mean coverage theorem binds the terminal
fixture without assuming a common terminal channel or its own conclusion.
Retained-margin arithmetic and null rejection have separate premise sets.
The terminal fixture binds all 16 campaign members times hardware/replay to
32 means. Formal indices 0 through 3 are hardware no_feed_forward s1/s3,
then replay no_feed_forward s1/s3. Their counts are 2964, 4911, 4067, 4057,
each divided by 8192. All 16 hardware PUBs require 8192 raw bitstrings,
matching register maps and exact collected terminal summaries. All 16 replay
count vectors require the recorded 8192-shot batch identity and member/PUB
mapping. Their rounded probability entries determine unique counts on that
shot grid, and the exact record, collection and per-replica terminal summaries
must agree. Ordered replay bitstrings were not retained; replay count recovery
and the physical IID premise remain distinct evidence surfaces.
The family validates every covered member and rejects
a reordered selected mean, changed digest, missing register, altered shot
count, changed semantic class or omitted premise.

The branch-signed decoder uses little-endian classical bits c0=branch,
c1=work_a, c2=work_b, c3=spectator. X uses H; Y uses Sdg followed by H.
Standard Y is [[0,-i],[i,0]]. For trace(rho O), the odd matrix element is
rho[01,10]=(XX+YY+i*(XY-YX))/4. Subnormalized branch matrices contribute
with their signed weights; renormalizing each branch changes the estimator.
Spectator marginalization permits correlation with every retained register.

Certified terminal concentration and multiplicity
-------------------------------------------------
FiniteProbability.v proves finite weighted Markov and union bounds.
FiniteBernoulliConcentration.v proves a degree-ten centered Bernoulli moment
bound using recursive finite convolution, then applies Markov and a union
bound to 32 means with 8192 samples each. The exact radius is 21/1000.
The certified family failure bound is below 1/20 (about 0.04822313).
The historical floating Hoeffding radius 0.02089696308600546 remains an
analysis quantity; the new certificate uses the conservative radius 0.021.

TerminalChannelEmpiricalCertificate.v composes simultaneous coverage with
the common binary stochastic terminal-channel null. A false rejection
requires a radius failure, so its pre-data probability is at most 1/20.
The exact observed separation margin after subtracting four radii is
156109/1024000 = 0.1524501953125. The probability statement describes the
sampling experiment under the null; fixed observed counts do not receive a
posterior null probability from that theorem.

The concentration premise requires the full marginal law of each centered
sum to equal the declared IID Bernoulli convolution. Within-mean stationarity,
shot independence and decoder correctness remain empirical assumptions.
The finite union bound permits arbitrary dependence across the 32 means.
The rejection excludes the stated common terminal channel model; readout
calibration, gate error, drift, crosstalk, leakage and context-dependent noise
remain alternative mechanisms outside that null.

Signed coherence and nuisance boundaries
----------------------------------------
BranchSignedCoherence.v proves Pauli trace reconstruction, signed decoder
parity, spectator partial trace, branch assignment attenuation, ordered Z
rotation, nuisance enclosures, and additive-drift nonidentifiability.
The phase order is theta_b-theta_a. A symmetric independent branch flip
model attenuates signed coherence by 1-2*epsilon. A balanced control-ZZ
mixture attenuates it by cos(coupling), under the stated mixture model.
A rotation of the measured complex coefficient alone identifies no cause.

The finite Born score identity is algebraic. Physical Born probabilities
require positive branch matrices whose combined trace is one; separately
subnormalized blocks alone do not establish normalization of their sum.
The assignment mixing identity holds algebraically for real epsilon; a
physical error probability requires 0 <= epsilon <= 1 and the independent
error model. The factorization predicate is declared separately, and the
source does not derive mix_matrix from that predicate. These physical links
remain premises rather than discharged operator theorems.

The three reconstructed complex coefficients are immediate
(0.413330078125,-0.006103515625), matched_idle
(0.15478515625,-0.34130859375), and measure_reset
(0.17724609375,-0.341064453125). The raw fringe counts are dark

comparison remains unresolved. Exploratory intervals can depend on unsigned
control bias and cross-job drift. Recorded-branch coherence concerns work
qubits within classical branches, rather than coherence between branches.
